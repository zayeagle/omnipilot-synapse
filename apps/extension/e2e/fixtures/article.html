Spaced repetition for people who hate flash cards

Reviewing material near the point of forgetting is the most efficient schedule, because the effort of retrieval is what strengthens the memory.

A practical rule is to rate every review honestly. Marking something as remembered when it was only familiar is the fastest way to build a review queue that lies to you.

  • Schedule by retrievability, not by a fixed calendar.
  • Shorten the interval after a miss.
  • Let frequently recalled material drift further away.
